Important Indian Judgements

Landmark Supreme Court Judgements Every Citizen Should Know

🔒 Right to Privacy

Puttaswamy Case (2017)

The Supreme Court declared privacy as a fundamental right under Article 21 of the Constitution.

Impact: Strengthened citizen privacy rights.

👩 Vishaka Guidelines

Vishaka vs State of Rajasthan

Established guidelines to prevent sexual harassment at workplaces.

Impact: Improved workplace safety for women.

🏛 Kesavananda Bharati Case

Basic Structure Doctrine

Parliament cannot alter the basic structure of the Constitution.

Impact: Protected constitutional values.

⚖ Shayara Bano Case

Triple Talaq Judgment

The Supreme Court declared instant Triple Talaq unconstitutional.

Impact: Enhanced women's rights.

📚 Right to Education

Article 21A

Education is recognized as a fundamental right for children.

Impact: Improved access to education.

🗳 NOTA Judgment

People's Union for Civil Liberties

Introduced the NOTA option in elections.

Impact: Increased voter choice.
