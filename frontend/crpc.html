What To Do In Emergency?

Cyber Fraud

Immediately call 1930 and report the fraud to prevent further transactions.

Theft

Visit the nearest police station and file an FIR as soon as possible.

Women Safety

Contact 1091 or emergency services if immediate assistance is required.
