Crime Categories

Learn about different crimes, punishments, citizen rights and reporting procedures.

💻 Cyber Crime

Cyber crimes involve illegal activities performed using computers, networks or the internet.

  • UPI Fraud
  • OTP Scam
  • Online Hacking
  • Identity Theft

Report At: Cyber Crime Portal / Helpline 1930

🚗 Theft

Theft is the unlawful taking of someone's property without permission.

  • Vehicle Theft
  • Mobile Theft
  • Property Theft

File FIR immediately at nearest police station.

⚠ Threats & Blackmail

Threatening, blackmailing or intimidating someone is punishable by law.

  • Verbal Threats
  • Physical Threats
  • Blackmail

👩 Women Safety

Women have special legal protections against harassment and violence.

  • Stalking
  • Harassment
  • Domestic Violence
  • Workplace Harassment

💰 Financial Fraud

Financial fraud includes cheating, loan harassment and investment scams.

  • Loan Harassment
  • Bank Fraud
  • Investment Scam

🚓 Police & Legal Issues

Know your legal rights during police investigation and arrest.

  • FIR Refusal
  • Illegal Arrest
  • Police Misconduct
