Crime Categories

Learn about different crimes, punishments, citizen rights and reporting procedures.

💻 Cyber Crime

Cyber crimes involve illegal activities performed using computers, networks or the internet.

  • UPI Fraud
  • OTP Scam
  • Online Hacking
  • Identity Theft

Report At: Cyber Crime Portal / Helpline 1930

🚗 Theft

Theft is the unlawful taking of someone's property without permission.

  • Vehicle Theft
  • Mobile Theft
  • Property Theft

File FIR immediately at nearest police station.

⚠ Threats & Blackmail

Threatening, blackmailing or intimidating someone is punishable by law.

  • Verbal Threats
  • Physical Threats
  • Blackmail

👩 Women Safety

Women have special legal protections against harassment and violence.

  • Stalking
  • Harassment
  • Domestic Violence
  • Workplace Harassment

💰 Financial Fraud

Financial fraud includes cheating, loan harassment and investment scams.

  • Loan Harassment
  • Bank Fraud
  • Investment Scam

🚓 Police & Legal Issues

Know your legal rights during police investigation and arrest.

  • FIR Refusal
  • Illegal Arrest
  • Police Misconduct

🔪 Murder

Murder is the intentional killing of another person and is one of the most serious criminal offences.

  • Intentional Homicide
  • Planned Murder
  • Contract Killing

Immediately report the incident to the nearest police station or dial 112.

👶 Child Abuse

Child abuse includes physical, emotional, sexual abuse and neglect of children.

  • Physical Abuse
  • Sexual Abuse
  • Child Labour
  • Neglect

Report to Child Helpline 1098 or the nearest police station.

💊 Drug Crime

Illegal possession, sale, manufacture or trafficking of narcotic drugs is punishable by law.

  • Drug Trafficking
  • Drug Possession
  • Illegal Sale

Report suspicious activities to the local police immediately.

🏠 Property Disputes

Property disputes involve ownership conflicts, illegal possession and land encroachment.

  • Land Disputes
  • Illegal Occupation
  • Forgery of Property Documents

Consult a legal expert and maintain proper property records.

🚨 Human Trafficking

Human trafficking involves recruiting or transporting people through force, fraud or coercion.

  • Forced Labour
  • Child Trafficking
  • Sex Trafficking

Immediately report suspected trafficking to the police or emergency number 112.

🧾 Document Forgery

Creating or using fake documents such as identity cards, certificates or property papers is an offence.

  • Fake Aadhaar
  • Fake Passport
  • Fake Certificates

Report forged documents to the police and the concerned authority.

🚦 Road Accident

Road accidents require immediate medical assistance and reporting to the police when appropriate.

  • Hit and Run
  • Drunk Driving
  • Overspeeding

Call 108 for medical emergencies and 112 if police assistance is needed.

🌐 Social Media Crime

Misuse of social media includes fake accounts, cyber bullying, online harassment and misinformation.

  • Fake Profile
  • Cyber Bullying
  • Online Defamation
  • Fake News

Report such incidents on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or call 1930.

💼 Workplace Harassment

Employees are protected against sexual harassment, discrimination and workplace bullying.

  • Sexual Harassment
  • Mental Harassment
  • Discrimination

Report the matter to your Internal Committee (IC) or the appropriate authority.

🏦 Corruption & Bribery

Offering or accepting illegal gratification by public servants or private individuals is punishable.

  • Bribery
  • Abuse of Office
  • Misuse of Public Funds

Report corruption to the competent anti-corruption authority in your state.
